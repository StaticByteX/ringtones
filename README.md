# 📢 **StaticByteX — Retro Ringtones Reloaded**

Bring the sound of the Commodore 64, Amiga 500, and early PC era straight to your modern phone.

StaticByteX is a curated collection of **ringtones and notification sounds** inspired by the legendary game, demoscene, and tracker era — lovingly remade for today's devices.

***

## 🎶 What You Get

*   Authentic **C64 SID‑style**, **Amiga Paula‑style**, and **AdLib/Sound Blaster‑style** tones — plus classic **Windows‑era** sounds
*   **Hundreds of tracks** — and growing
*   Instant, correctly named downloads: **MP3** (Android) and **M4R** (iPhone)
*   Platform‑correct colors + tiny retro **C64 / A500 / DOS / WIN** logos
*   Always free. No ads. No nonsense.

***

## ▶️ Built‑In Player

*   Tap the **play button** on any track — it plays instantly in a sleek bottom player bar
*   **Now playing** info with title and composer, in platform colors
*   One shared player for the whole library — smooth even on older phones

***

## 🔍 Find Your Sound Fast

*   **Search** across titles, composers, handles, groups, productions, publishers, years, and tags
*   **Filter** by platform (C64 / AMIGA / DOS / WIN)
*   **Filter** by type (Ringtones / Notifications)
*   **Sort** by title, handle, name, group, production, or publisher
*   Live result counts for tracks, composers, productions, and publishers

***

## 📱 Designed for Mobile

*   Buttery‑smooth scrolling through the full library — even on iPhone
*   Each phone sees only its own download button: **Android** gets MP3, **iPhone** gets M4R
*   Built‑in **iPhone guide**: a short GarageBand walkthrough pops up the first time you download, and is always available via the ⓘ button
*   Works great in Safari, Chrome, **and Firefox** on iOS — downloads keep their proper filename and `.m4r` extension
*   Centered, touch‑friendly filter buttons

***

## 🎼 Clean Metadata

Every track is presented in a clear, consistent format:

*   **Handle (Real Name) · Group** — parentheses always mean the composer's real name
*   **Production · Publisher · Year** — with duplicates automatically removed
*   Original sources credited as: `Based on <Title> · <Artist> · <Year>`

***

## 📦 Technology

*   Static front‑end (HTML, CSS, JavaScript) — no frameworks, no build step
*   JSON‑driven track data, pure client‑side
*   Lazy‑loading cards + a single shared audio player for effortless scrolling
*   Optimized assets for a fast first load

***
